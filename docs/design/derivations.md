# Derivations

`AlgorithmTrace` is low-level developer diagnostics. It is unstable and is not
a user-facing mathematical explanation.

`MathematicalDerivation` is semantic, structured, deterministic, and built as
mathematical computation proceeds. It can be rendered independently of the
algorithm. A future step will record before and after statements, a stable
`RuleId`, the mathematical relation, required assumptions, introduced
restrictions, proof or verification obligations, and optional sub-derivations.

Relation concepts include `Equal`, `Equivalent`, `Implies`, `Approximate`, and
`DefinedAs`. Phrases such as “By the zero-product property” belong to rendering,
not mathematical semantics. Mathematishia is not claiming to be a formal proof
assistant.

This derivation engine is not implemented yet.
