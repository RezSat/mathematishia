# Licensing and provenance

This is an engineering policy, not legal advice. Mathematishia is licensed
under Apache-2.0. Permissively licensed dependencies are preferred for the
initial core, and dependency and source provenance should be reviewed before
substantial third-party code is introduced.

Do not copy, translate, port, or vendor GPL implementation code into the
Apache-2.0 core. Public mathematical algorithms and ideas may be implemented
independently from legitimate references while recording source provenance.

LGPL/native libraries such as FLINT, GMP, and MPFR may be technically usable
later; distribution and linking obligations require deliberate review before
shipping them as part of Mathematishia.
